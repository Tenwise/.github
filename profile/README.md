<p align="center"><strong>Read widely. Keep what matters.</strong></p>

Tenwise is a feed reader with a research desk built in. Follow your sources, save what's worth keeping with a note on why, and cite it when you write. It's made by CodeStrata, Inc.

## Run it yourself: Tenwise Engine

[**tenwise-engine**](https://github.com/Tenwise/tenwise-engine) is the open-source core of Tenwise, under the GNU AGPL v3. It's a local-first RSS reader that runs on your own computer:

- Dense rows to scan, a calm docked reader, and a key for everything
- Saves with notes, collections and ready-made APA citations
- AI summaries and chat on your device, with Gemini Nano in Chrome or Ollama
- No account, no cloud and no tracking: your library is one SQLite file

[Quick start](https://github.com/Tenwise/tenwise-engine#quick-start) · [Contributing](https://github.com/Tenwise/tenwise-engine/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Security](https://github.com/Tenwise/tenwise-engine/blob/main/SECURITY.md)

## Or use it hosted: tenwise.app

[tenwise.app](https://tenwise.app) is the app built on the engine, by the same team. Sign in with your email, with nothing to install, and your library is there in any browser.

[User guide](https://tenwise.app/docs/) · [ask@tenwise.app](mailto:ask@tenwise.app)
